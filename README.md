# news-image-20261124 — "The World Today", Tuesday 24 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page that sums up the world news of
**Tuesday, 24 November 2026** in a single 1024×1024 image:

- **Lead — Ukraine:** a Russian overnight strike on Zaporizhzhia kills 2
  people and wounds 22, including two children (Reuters).
- **Asia · Security:** South Korea scrambles F-15K jets as Russian military
  aircraft enter its air defence zone; Ulleung Island's shelter alert is
  lifted within minutes, with no airspace breach (Reuters, AP, Yonhap).
- **South Asia · Politics:** ousted prime minister Sheikh Hasina announces
  she will return to Bangladesh from exile in India on December 16; a
  government minister warns she faces arrest (Reuters, AP).
- **US · NATO:** President Trump will meet NATO chief Mark Rutte in Palm
  Beach on Wednesday as Europe pushes for a seat in the US-led Ukraine
  peace effort (Reuters).
- **Indonesia · Disaster:** rescuers still search for dozens missing after
  the Cikande factory fire, with 24 people confirmed dead (press reports).
- **Economy:** a Reuters poll expects South Korea's central bank to raise
  rates to 3.25% on Thursday amid the chip-led export boom.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the bundled `entry.png`
to `/output/entry.png`. The image was composed with a Python/PIL script
from wire reporting cross-checked across at least two sources per story.
